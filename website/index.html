Gym Plus

Your workouts. Your weights. Your data.

A free, open-source gym & body-weight tracker for your phone. Works fully offline — sync to your own Google Drive and an AI coach are optional extras.

Get Gym Plus Try the demo

Everything a training log needs

Built in the gym, not in a boardroom

Every feature exists because it was needed on the gym floor — nothing is there to sell you anything.

☁️

Optional Google Drive sync

Sign in with Google and your log is kept in a Gym Plus folder in your own Drive. Skip sign-in and everything stays on the phone.

🤖

AI coach, your key

Bring your own OpenAI, Claude, Gemini, Groq or OpenRouter key. It stays in the phone's secure storage and is never synced.

▶️

Guided workouts

Knows what day it is, starts today's session, pre-fills your weights from last time, times your rest, spots PRs.

🏋️

1,324 exercises

Searchable library with animated demos, filterable by body part and the equipment you actually own — plus your own custom exercises.

🗓️

Weekly plan

A routine per weekday, and any day can be rescheduled when life happens — without touching the plan.

⚖️

Body weight & goal

Interactive chart with your goal line; every change is colored by whether it moves toward it.

💪

Muscle map

A body diagram shaded by how much work each muscle got — and which ones got nothing.

🟩

Activity heatmap

A GitHub-style year view of your training time. Streaks included.

📈

Progression that follows a rule

Linear, Greyskull LP, double progression or added time — per routine, overridable per exercise. Your weights are already right when the session opens, and every target tells you why.

⏱️

Timed sets

Planks, hangs and carries are logged by time, with a timer for the set itself. Stop early and it logs what you actually held.

🧮

Estimated 1RM

Per exercise, from your best eligible set — and it names which one. Won't guess above 12 reps.

🔗

Supersets & cardio

Pair exercises back-to-back with one rest, and log time + speed where weight × reps makes no sense.

📤

Share a plan

Send someone your routines as a small file — no workouts, no weigh-ins — or print it as a clean PDF. Importing merges, never overwrites.

🎯

Effort per set

Rate a set as RIR or RPE — whichever scale you already think in. Optional, and every set keeps the scale it was logged with.

📥

Bring your history

Import from FitNotes, Strong, Hevy — including the RPE they recorded — or body weight straight out of an Apple Health export.

🌍

12 languages

Full UI translation, exercise instructions in 10 languages, loaded on demand.

🎨

Designed, not assembled

Light & dark themes, 8 accent colors, a hand-drawn icon set. Looks the same on every phone.

📦

Yours to keep

One-tap JSON export and import. Your training log is a file you own, not a row in someone's cloud.

Get Gym Plus

One app, two flavors

Same codebase, your choice of where the data lives: on your phone (optionally mirrored to your own Google Drive), or on a server you control.

📱 Mobile app (Android & iOS)

For anyone — install and you're done.

  • Works fully offline, no account needed
  • Optional Google sign-in → sync via your own Drive
  • Optional AI coach with your own API key
  • Native workout-day reminders
  • Backups via the share sheet
Install / build guide

Package com.gympulse.app. Release builds are not published on this site yet — see Build it yourself. The APK at the upstream openGym site is openGym, not Gym Plus.

🖥️ Self-hosted

For homelabbers — and their friends & family.

  • Sync across all your devices
  • Passkey profiles for multiple people
  • Your data readable on the desktop
  • Works as a home-screen app on iPhone & Android
  • One docker compose up

AGPL-3.0

Standing on openGym

Gym Plus is based on openGym by Duarte Santos — a tracker that started as a tiny app he built for himself, then grew into an AGPL open-source project. Gym Plus adds a mobile-first build with Drive sync and a bring-your-own-key coach.

Open source

AGPL-licensed, forever

Gym Plus is free software under the AGPL-3.0, like the openGym project it is built on. The license keeps every fork open, and there is no commercial version waiting behind a curtain.
